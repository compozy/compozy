## Scope and limitations

This targeted round covers the changed offline recovery and failed-job bell behavior plus adjacent bell canaries. It does not repeat every historical variant listed in the scenario documents. Monitor filtering, profile/actor receipt isolation and population beyond the display cap retain their focused Go test evidence. Live third-party providers, operating-system notifications and off-app delivery are outside this pass. The web build used the same Vite pipeline with minification disabled to fit local build memory.

Screenshots and traces were captured by the existing BrowserArtifactSession collector. Evidence is under `docs/qa/evidence/2026-11-18-attention-pr1820/`, following the repository's evidence sidecar convention. No production credentials or operator data were used.
