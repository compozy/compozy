## Evidence expectations

- Cross-plane absence matrix, native catalog and official-skill prompt capture, three-kind Marketplace
  comparison, zero-residue reads, reserved-name conflict, protected-resource rejection, support archive
  receipt, and bundled-skill structured output.

## Selection rationale

Targeted tier owner for Safety Invariants 11–15 and ADR-001 and ADR-006. The support and
`--source bundled` checks are boundary canaries inside this session, not standalone cases.
