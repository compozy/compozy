T8S5 banner stripBetween the menubar and the win-layer, full-width, flat canvas-soft, neutral signal — it pushes the work area down and never overlays window content. Wraps to two lines at 390px.desktop-shell.tsx (already placed here — ratified)
T9Dock pager · real-device deltaThe desktop pager must not lead the touch dock: with a single desktop it rendered an orphaned position pill beside a 50vw dead zone (the "estranho" spacing). Compact renders the pager only when a second desktop exists, shrink-wrapped and capped at half the bar; the floating dock keeps the position pill at every count.desktop-pager.tsx
T10Window-control identification · real-device deltaCompact controls carry their identification glyph at rest — close (X), minimize (−), in the same neutral ink; hover tones remain the hover/focus affordance. The 2-of-3 count is per-design: compact hides zoom (meaningless in a stack), never close — a phone closes a window from its own bar. Inert chrome stays glyphless (no implied controls).os-traffic-lights.tsx

Touch-tier boundary. ≤760px is the repo's established touch floor (dialog hosts, help tips); the shell's compact presentation boundary stays 960px. Between 760–960 the compact layout renders with desktop target sizes — tablet landscape in UI-13 keeps its current shape, so >1024px rendering stays pixel-identical by construction.

Residuals, recorded: the profile-switcher trigger keeps its 28px well (cross-system slot in systems/profiles, quiet-until-plural — a follow-up if the QA walk flags it); log bodies keep their own horizontal scroll wells inside the read view (extension log panel is a read view composed of existing grammar; no change).
