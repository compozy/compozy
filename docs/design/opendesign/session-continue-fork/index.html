Session continue & fork

Three boards for .compozy/tasks/session-continue-fork/_uiux.md (S1–S10). Two verbs join the places secondary session verbs already live — the row kebab and the window's More actions — and one verb joins the user message: Continue with another agent…, Fork session…, Fork from here. Each opens a small dialog on the Start-session shell that shows what will be carried over before you decide and where the new session opens. The child says where it came from with a neutral pill, a hairline at the top of its transcript, and an Origin row in its ledger; the source is never touched and says nothing. Every board opens with what it is, which production components it changes, and the states it covers with their Visual Contract ids. The semantic contract is DESIGN-NOTES.md.

How to read a board
1 · Contract blockThree columns at the top: the surface in plain words · the production files it modifies, adds, deletes, or reuses unchanged · the states on the page, each linked, each with its VC id.
2 · Callouts + legendInverted numbers next to the element; the legend under the specimen names the component, the token values, and the authority: production · delta · truth.
3 · Tag stripstate · component · VC · tone/accent per specimen, so a VC id greps straight to its specimen (data-od-id="scf-vc-NN").
