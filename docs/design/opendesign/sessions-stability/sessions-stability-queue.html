What this board is

S2 — the queue as a place you manage. Parked follow-ups sit in a strip fused to the top of the composer, one row each: position, who queued it, a one-line preview, and the three verbs. A send whose acknowledgment got lost shows as “not confirmed” with a Retry that replays the same identity, so it can never duplicate. Interrupt leaves the queue alone; emptying it is its own explicit action, and every removed entry leaves a trace in the conversation.
