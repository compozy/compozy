What this board is

S5 + S6 + S7 — the conversation reads as a narrative, not a machine log. While a turn runs there is exactly one live tool row that replaces itself; finished tools of that turn collapse into one line; when the turn settles its work folds behind “Worked for 4m 12s · Ran 6 commands, edited 2 files” and the answer stays. A turn you stopped stays open where you stopped it. Scrolling has one owner at a time, and streamed prose reveals smoothly.

Scroll has one owner — following, anchoring a new turn, or you

US-024 · ADR-007 · S6
Interaction contract, drawn on the transcript. Following: pinned to the end, no pill. You scroll up one notch: following disengages at once and the production jump pill appears; the stream never tugs the viewport. Re-arm happens only inside the bottom band — 40px, not today’s 96 — or by the pill. After you send, your bubble anchors 16px under the top and the anchor accounts for the composer’s real height (today it assumes 0). Loading older history keeps the exact reading position; folds and composer growth never move it; steering an already-streaming turn snaps instead of animating.

Streaming prose — smooth, snaps at the end, off when you say so

US-025 · ADR-008 · task_07 VC-09
Presentation only — the transcript is never delayed. Chunks arrive in bursts (~100ms); the reveal drains each burst over ~160ms at a velocity that tracks arrival (capped at 2000 chars/s), and snaps to the full text the moment the stream ends. Reduced motion and the Smooth streaming setting (S10) both render chunks directly. Code blocks re-highlight on a separate, slower throttle so big pastes never stall a frame. Press Replay to watch the three modes on the same paragraph.
