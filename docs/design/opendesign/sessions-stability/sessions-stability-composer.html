What this board is

S1 — the composer while an agent works. One primary control that is Send when the session is idle, Stop while a turn runs, and a truthful “Stopping…” until the runtime confirms. Enter follows your follow-up default (steer); ⌘⏎ does the opposite once. Every busy send answers inside the card with what happened to it, and a refused send gives your draft back.

Refused — the reason, and your draft back

US-004.AC-3 · US-004.EC-1 · task_01 VC-05

The warning glyph is the only tone: a gate is not a failure of the system. The hint updates to the new truth — if the turn ended meanwhile, it now reads send.

06

Stopping — the disc widens into a word and waits for the truth

US-009.AC-1 · ADR-004 · task_02 VC-01
Stop is a contract, so the control shows the in-between state. One press: the disc becomes a quiet “Stopping…” pill with a spinner, guarded against a second activation. The status row and the head dot say the same thing. Steer and Interrupt disappear — the turn is ending, they can’t be honored — while Queue stays, because queued work survives a stop (ADR-003) and Enter now queues. The cooperative phase has a budget (session.stop.cooperative_grace = 10s); after it the runtime escalates on its own, and the pill keeps saying Stopping until the daemon confirms.
