OS Shell — Window Manager

The shell is flat chrome around a desk of gutterless tiles: a full-width 52px topbar whose first 84px are reserved for the window's traffic lights, a 60px rail on the left (the dock), and the desk, where windows tile edge to edge, split by a single hairline. There is no glass and no radius waiver: chrome sits on --rail, panes on --canvas, and only floating windows, menus and popovers lift. Every value here is production truth from web/src/systems/os and packages/ui/src/tokens.css, expressed as the ds-shell.css classes. The design reference is shell-rail/shell-rail-v2.html, which supersedes the dock-era edition of this chapter. Use Theme (or ?theme=light): every specimen reads in both themes.

01Anatomy

Three zones, one grid: the topbar spans the full width, the rail sits below it on the left, and the desk fills the rest. The desk is the flat --desk role by default; windows tile with zero gaps, so the seam hairline is the only divider between panes.

02Topbar (the menubar)

One flat 52px strip on --rail with a hairline under it (aria-label="System bar"). Leading is where you are: the traffic-light reserve, the mark, the Global scope globe, the project chip and the app menus. Trailing is the tray: desktop pager, All desktops, the bell and ⌘K. Settings is not in the topbar: it lives in the rail foot and in the mark's menu.

03Rail (the dock)

The dock is a 60px rail on the left, under the topbar. In product copy it stays the dock (aria-label="Dock", "Open an app from the dock"); "rail" only names its shape. Launchers follow the dock order with no dividers; the foot holds the profile switcher, the theme toggle and Settings.

Sessions ⌘1 Light mode

Tooltips sit to the right (offset 10) as an inverted pill. Up/Down rove the launchers (wrapping), Home/End jump. The context menu offers Open in new window, Open as tab in focused window and Go to tab. The theme toggle shows a sun while dark and a moon while light ("Switch to light mode" / "Switch to dark mode").

05The deck: browser tabs

At two or more tabs the window grows a 40px deck: a recessed --sunken strip with the window controls on the right. The active tab is a surface plate with rounded top corners and concave feet, fused with the head below it; inactive tabs are split by hairlines that hide next to hover and next to the active tab.

06Tiling, seams & arrange

Gutterless: tiled panes meet edge to edge (gaps default 0) and share one 1px --line seam with a 9px hit area. Hover, focus or drag turns it into a 2px accent line. Arrow keys move a focused seam 2%, Shift 10%; each pane keeps at least 220px. New windows tile beside the focused window.

07Desktops, overview & wallpapers

Desktops are switched from the pager dots in the tray; the All desktops button opens the Desktops overview, which owns create, rename, reorder, move and delete. Every desktop stays mounted. Switching hides and inerts it, so scroll, focus and streams survive.

Pager

6px dots in 24×30 buttons; the active desktop is an 18px --fg pill; an orange dot marks an off-screen desktop that needs you. Left/Right/Home/End move; above 7 desktops the pager windows ±2 with "Show N earlier/later desktops".

08Themes

Two themes, dark by default. The preference is Light / Dark / System in Settings › Appearance ("System follows your computer's light or dark setting."); the rail-foot toggle flips the painted theme and stores an explicit choice. One function sets data-theme and the .dark class together on <html> before first paint; the terminal follows the theme.

11Shell rules

S1Identity renders once. The 48px head owns it: root identity, breadcrumb, or document self-title, never two stacked and never a second route header inside the body.
S2Chrome is bounded. Deck 40 + head 48 + toolbar 44 = 132px fully loaded. Anything beyond belongs in the body.
S3Views live on the toolbar as its leading pills: views · filters · spacer · display. The in-head .w2-tabs variant is deprecated.
S4One owner per axis. Depth is the breadcrumb, project is the topbar chip, apps are the dock, desktops are the pager. No zone borrows another's job.
S5Documents self-title with a state glyph and never breadcrumb.
S6Flat chrome, no glass. The topbar and rail sit on --rail; tiled panes are flat on --canvas. Only floating windows (shadow-elevated), menus and popovers (shadow-pop) lift.
S7Minimize folds into the dock item (hollow ring + dimmed glyph). No minimize tray, no desktop widgets; approvals live in the bell.
S8Settings opens from the rail foot (and the mark's menu). It never takes a launcher slot and never sits in the tray.
S9Orange means needs you. Badges, the pager's attention dot, the seam under your hand, snap previews. The primary action is inverted, amber is only a real warning, and tab selection is neutral.
S10Gutterless tiling. Gaps default to 0; the 1px seam is the only divider and its 9px hit area is the only resize handle between panes.
S11State persists per desktop via the daemon snapshot — layout tree, tab set and order, per-tab nav stacks, wallpaper. The theme is the one per-browser preference (compozy.theme).
S12Tiled is not floating. Tiled panes drop border and shadow, resize only through seams, and stay inside the layout tree.
S13Both themes, always. Every shell specimen reads in light and dark; a literal that only works on one theme is a defect (lint: no-design-glaze-rgba).
