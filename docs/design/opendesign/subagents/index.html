Subagents

Three boards for .compozy/tasks/subagents/_uiux.md (S1–S10) and ADR-005. A delegation becomes a live card in the parent's transcript, and cards from the same turn fold into a group. A new HoverCard primitive carries the details the row leaves out. When the parent's turn ends, a strip on the composer says who it is still waiting on. In the sidebar, a parent with ten subagents stays one row with a 3/10 chip, and the full roster lives in the inspector. Every board opens with what it is, which production components it changes, and the states it covers with their Visual Contract ids. The semantic contract is DESIGN-NOTES.md.
