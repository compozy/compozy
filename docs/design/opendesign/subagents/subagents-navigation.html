What this board is

Ten subagents, still one row. Subagent sessions stay out of the sidebar list, so they never push other sessions off the page. Instead, the parent row gets a small chip: the most urgent state, and how many are live out of the total (3/10). Hovering it previews the top five. Clicking it opens the full list in the session's inspector.

The inspector's new Subagents section lists live ones first, keeps finished ones in a folded group, pages long lists, and can stop a live one from its row.
