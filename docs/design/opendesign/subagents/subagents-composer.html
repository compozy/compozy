What this board is

The parent's turn ended, but its subagents are still working. Today the composer goes quiet, so the session looks finished. A strip on top of the composer now says who the parent is waiting on, lets you jump to any of them, and stops them all with one button.

While the turn is still running, the status line under the transcript counts every live subagent, including the provider's own agents.
