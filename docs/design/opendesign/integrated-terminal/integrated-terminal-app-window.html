Terminal window — you control

US-002.AC-1 · US-005 · US-010.AC-1
The dark screen is the window. Tabs list this project's terminals; each one gets a 44px head — the name once, a chip saying who's in control, who's watching — and at most two trailing actions. The screen sits full-bleed on --term-bg, one step darker than the canvas. A solid cursor means you can type. Nothing is highlighted at rest — being in control is the quiet default, not an event. Read-only log — output, not a prompt

US-013 · exec-yield promotion
Born from a command, never interactive. A long-running command becomes a read-only log: the tab wears the log glyph, the pane shows numbered lines, and there is nothing to type into — no cursor, no control actions, no record. What remains is honest: wait, signal, close. Typing here isn't a disabled option; it doesn't exist. US-013 · terminal_not_interactive
