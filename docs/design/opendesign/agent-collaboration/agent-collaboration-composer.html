What this board is

A message that is waiting in the queue already says who sent it. When the target session is busy, another agent's message parks in the queue strip above the composer. Its row shows the sender's provider mark and "From {title}" where other actors' rows show their owner.

You can remove it, but you can't edit or steer it: the text is another agent's, and changing it would break who sent it and the reply it is waiting for. Removing it tells a waiting sender the message was dropped.

Refusal on other surfaces

VC-05
