A AGH Runtime·Loops overview

Loops

A Loop is a reusable unit of self-correcting work: a contract (goal, verification, stop conditions), a body of steps laid out as a static DAG, and typed inputs. It runs unattended on AGH's autonomy kernel, iterating across generations until the goal is verified, or it stops honestly at one of six terminal outcomes. The workspace stays simple: two nouns.
