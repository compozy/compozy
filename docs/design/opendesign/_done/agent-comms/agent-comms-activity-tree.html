The nine states a call can be in

task_06/VC-02
Color, icon, and word always travel togetherGreen means it worked, red means it needs your look, amber means someone stopped it on purpose. Waiting and working stay gray — the moving dots, not a color, say something is alive. The word in the chip is the runtime's exact term, so screen and CLI never disagree. US-001 · US-005 · US-008 · US-019
queuedneutralIn line, waiting to start. Nothing waits invisibly — even when the system is busy, the wait shows up here.
runningneutralThe helper is working right now. The moving dots show it — color never does.
completedsuccessThe answer came back and matched what was asked. The small gray word beside it says how it arrived.
invalid-resultdangerThe answer didn't match what was asked, even after one retry. Both tries are kept for you to read.
completed-without-resultdangerThe helper finished but sent nothing back. Needs your look, same as a wrong answer.
faileddangerSomething broke along the way. The reason is kept with the record.
canceledwarningSomeone stopped it on purpose. An answer arriving late is kept as evidence — it never reopens the call.
timeoutwarningIt ran past a deadline someone chose to set. No deadline set, no timer anywhere.
expireddangerThe helper sat idle too long and left. Ask the agent again to start fresh.
A helper is working, resting, or goneA resting helper (moon) is still reachable — writing to it or asking again is what brings it back, so there is no Revive button anywhere. A gone helper keeps its history; its buttons are simply absent, never grayed out. US-018.AC-3
runningneutralBusy with an assignment right now.
parkedneutralFinished and resting, still reachable. The idle clock only runs while resting.
goneneutralLeft for good. The row keeps who it was; the buttons are simply not there.
