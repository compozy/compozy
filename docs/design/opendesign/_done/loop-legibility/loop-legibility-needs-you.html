the set's one tone map
Tone never travels aloneEvery state pairs a tone, a glyph and its literal word. Pending inks warning on the run page; danger is reserved for expired requests, failed and quarantined nodes, and the bell's re-ink. Absence — canceled, not taken, never materialised — stays on the neutral ramp. graph-eng lock · ADR-006
pending pending warning A human decision is outstanding on this run page.
pendingexpires 4m near-expiry warning Still warning, with the shared mono countdown. It never auto-answers.
expired expired danger The window closed. The loop settled without your answer.
answered answered / amended info Settled — here, from the CLI, or by another operator. The actor is named.
partial 7 of 10 partial warning A join settled short. The chip always carries the word and the coverage.
canceled canceled / not taken neutral Absence is calm. The cause and the actor live in the sentence beside it.
