## Recognize a new sign-in

The activation page shows the computer, platform, code, and the request's approximate location when available. Continue only if you started the sign-in on that computer.

When a new device first signs in, Compozy Cloud sends an email and lists the device in **Settings → Account** for seven days. You can revoke the device from Settings. The email also offers **Revoke this device** after a fresh Google sign-in with the account's verified email. **Dismiss** clears the currently displayed notices on that installation.

## Choose whether to get product news

Product news is off by default. You can opt in when you sign in or change your preference in **Settings → Account → Email**. An unsubscribe link opens a page where you can confirm. If your mail app offers a one-click unsubscribe action, it can apply the change directly. Security and account notices are sent independently of this preference.

## Delete your account

From an admin device, open **Settings → Account → Delete account** and confirm the account's email address. This signs that installation out immediately. Compozy Cloud finishes the deletion in the background, including unlinking computers and ending sign-in on other installations. Your local projects and sessions stay on your computers.

Deleting the account ends Compozy Pro immediately. An eligible first payment or annual renewal is fully refunded within the [14-day refund window](/legal/refunds).

If your account is suspended, contact support@compozy.com. After the suspension is lifted, a computer signed in through its link can recover at its next check. Sign in again on your other devices.
