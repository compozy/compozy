---
title: Remote Computers
description: Run coding agents across your laptops, desktops, Linux servers, and phone from one CompozyOS app.
icon: Monitor
---

Remote Computers lets one CompozyOS app drive agents across every machine you own. Add a Linux
server or desktop over SSH without installing the daemon by hand, choose where each new session runs
with **Run on**, follow sessions from every connected machine in one sidebar and board, and check in
from your phone browser over a Pro hosted route.

Sessions, prompts, files, and transcripts stay on the computer running the agent. Compozy Cloud
only coordinates sign-in, plan entitlements, and connection handshakes between your devices.

## Plans

| Plan     | What is included                                                                                                                  |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Free** | Every local feature, unlimited projects and sessions on your own computers, and **1 remote computer** over LAN or SSH.            |
| **Pro**  | Unlimited remote computers, **hosted routes** from any network (up to 10 at once), phone and browser access, and hosted webhooks. |

See [Pricing](/pricing) for monthly and annual rates, or [Manage Compozy Pro](/docs/account/billing)
for checkout, plan switching, and refunds.
