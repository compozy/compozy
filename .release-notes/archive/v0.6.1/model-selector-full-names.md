---
title: The model selector shows full model names
type: feat
---

Hover a model whose name is cut off in the model selector to see its full name, including the upstream provider shown under OpenCode models. When there are more providers than fit in the provider filter row, arrows at its edges page through them, and the row's targets are larger. The selector is the same in the message box, **Settings → General**, agent creation, continuing a session and Loops.

Migration notes: no action needed.
